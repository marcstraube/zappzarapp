# Code of Conduct

This document provides community guidelines for a safe, respectful, productive,
and collaborative place for any person who is willing to contribute to this
project. It applies to all collaborative spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other project communication channel.

- Participants will be tolerant of opposing views.
- Participants must ensure that their language and actions are free of personal
  attacks and disparaging personal remarks.
- When interpreting the words and actions of others, participants should always
  assume good intentions.
- Behaviour which can be reasonably considered harassment will not be tolerated.

If you experience or observe behaviour that violates these guidelines, contact
<email@marcstraube.de>.

Adapted from the
[Ruby Community Conduct Guideline](https://www.ruby-lang.org/en/conduct/).
